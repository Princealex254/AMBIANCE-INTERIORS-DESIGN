Our Story

Crafting soulful spaces that harmonize luxury with sustainability.

A Decade of Excellence

Founded in the heart of Nairobi and serving all major counties in Kenya, Ambiance Interiors Ltd began with a single vision: to transform the conventional Kenyan interior design landscape into one that prioritizes both human well-being and environmental integrity.

What started as a boutique consultancy has evolved into a full-service interior architecture and fit-out firm. We have spent the last 10 years curating a network of master artisans, sustainable material suppliers, and technical experts to deliver projects that are as functional as they are breathtaking.

Our philosophy is simple: we don't just design rooms; we design experiences. Every texture, light fixture, and spatial layout is chosen to evoke a sense of "Ambiance" that resonates with the soul of the inhabitant.

Our Mission

To deliver bespoke interior solutions that integrate luxury aesthetics with sustainable practices, enhancing the quality of life for our residential and corporate clients across East Africa.

Our Vision

To be the premier benchmark for soulful, eco-conscious interior design in Kenya, recognized for our innovation in spatial optimization and commitment to artisanal craftsmanship.

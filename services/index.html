Service FAQs

Quick answers to our most common service-related inquiries.

Do you provide turnkey project management?

Yes. We handle everything from the initial design and sourcing to contractor oversight and final styling, providing a stress-free experience for our clients.

Can I request only one service (e.g. just ceilings)?

Absolutely. While we love full transformations, we are happy to assist with specific elements like gypsum ceilings, cabinetry, or lighting consultancy.

Do you design for small apartments?

Yes. We specialize in space optimization, using clever joinery and lighting to make smaller spaces feel expansive and highly functional.

What is the typical timeframe for an office fit-out?

Most corporate fit-outs take between 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the scale and complexity of technical integrations required.
